01 Origin · Delhi, May

The Indian mother who knows her ingredient list

She loves her skincare routine. She reads the labels and when she became a mother, she didn't lower that standard. She carried it forward.

Gago began with one of those mothers. Our founder, Pragya, spent a year carrying around back the same Danish lotion from Copenhagen for her daughter — cleanly made, beautifully formulated, the kind of thing you don't think twice about in a Stockholm October.

Then May arrived in Delhi. The same lotion she had trusted all winter suddenly sat heavy — too rich for 38 degrees, written for a climate seven thousand kilometres away. The formula wasn't wrong. It was written for the wrong weather.

05 Questions

Ask us anything. We'll tell you.

When does Gago launch?+

Daily Dissolve ships in late 2026. Everyone on the waitlist gets 48-hour early access before public launch, and the first 500 sign-ups get founders' pricing on their first bottle.

Who is it for?+

Daily Dissolve is a single lotion for body and face. Light enough for newborn skin from day one, rigorous enough for the mother who reads ingredient lists. One bottle for the whole household.

Why "designed in Denmark, made for India"?+

The formula was developed in Copenhagen by a Danish cosmetic chemist with twenty years in barrier-friendly skincare, then tested and adapted for Indian climate and skin. It is manufactured to GMP standards in small, lot-traceable batches in Bangalore.

Why only one product?+

We don't launch ranges before we've earned them. One bottle, every day, for the whole family. When we add to it, it will be because something specific is missing — not because the line needs filling.

What's on the waitlist?+

Three things. A 48-hour early-access window before public launch. Founders' pricing on your first bottle for the first 500 sign-ups. And one short, useful update from us each month between now and launch — nothing else.

How much will it cost?+

Public pricing will be announced closer to launch. Founders' pricing is meaningfully lower and locked in for waitlist sign-ups #001–500. You'll know your number the moment you join.

Where will you ship?+

India first, at launch. International (UAE, Singapore, UK) shortly after. The waitlist is open globally — we'll tell you the moment we can ship to your address.
